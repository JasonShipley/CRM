This tool estimates airflow, static pressure and brake horsepower from standard duct-design and fan-similarity (affinity-law) equations for planning purposes. Air density correction is exact; the duct-friction, fitting-loss and no-reference efficiency figures are engineering estimates. Always confirm the final fan selection against the manufacturer's certified performance curve before purchase or installation.

Site Conditions

Standard air = 0.075 lb/ft³ at 70°F, sea level. Hot or high-altitude air is less dense — a fan must work harder (spin faster / draw more power) to hit the same actual static pressure.
