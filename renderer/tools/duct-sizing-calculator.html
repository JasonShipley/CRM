Why even sizes only

Air duct systems for grain and feed dust need a minimum of 4,000 CFM/ft² (4,000 FPM) to keep dust from settling in horizontal runs. Sawdust uses 4,500 CFM/ft². Always round a solved diameter up, never down — rounding down drops velocity below the floor. Avoid odd-diameter duct (9", 11", 13", 15") — even sizes are more available and cost much less.

Minimum CFM = duct area (ft²) × minimum velocity (FPM). Sizing only — confirm against system static pressure and fan curve before finalizing.
