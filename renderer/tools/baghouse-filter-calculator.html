Spec Reminders — Don't Quote Without These
  • Explosion venting: grain and wood dusts are combustible (NFPA). Vendor quotes carry vents below the bags, plus extra X-vents to cover the plenum on plenum-mounted units.
  • Housing rating: quoted filters are stressed to 17" w.g.
  • Utilities: compressed air per model shown above (80–100 PSI) + 110V single-phase for the pulse timer.
  • Bags/cages: 16 oz bags standard; top-removal (AST-style) or side/bottom-removal (AVS-style) per service access.
  • Full sizing basis: references/baghouse-filter-sizing.md
