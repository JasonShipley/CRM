Balance & pattern rules

Total hammer count must be even, and opposite rows (1&5, 2&6, 3&7, 4&8) must carry equal counts — the tool distributes total ÷ 2 across the row pairs as evenly as possible (pairs differ by at most one hammer).

Reference: the 38/44-40 corn pattern runs 132 hammers on 8 rows — pairs of 17, 17, 16, 16 per row — ≈198 HP at 1.5 HP/hammer.

HP per hammer is a starting estimate like the index number — confirm against factory experience for the specific product and grind.
