Sizing guidelines

Never over-horsepower a mill relative to its screen area — the product physically cannot exit the grinding chamber any faster no matter how much HP is applied. Excess HP with too little screen area causes hammer rock-back, plugging, excess heat, and faster wear.

Rotor family: 22"/44" mills are the typical pick for grain & biomass on 60 Hz power (20,735 FPM tip speed at 3600/1800 RPM). 19"/38" mills are the usual choice for rendering, or for 380V 50 Hz motor jobs where the motor speeds land the correct tip speed.

Free-flowing granular product (corn, cereal grains) → rotary feeder. Light/fibrous/bulky product that may bridge (wood fiber, biomass) → vibratory or screw feeder + separate magnet adapter.

Catalog cu ft/revolution feeder figures are rated at 100% pocket fill — this tool sizes rotary feeders at 90% pocket fill because it's not a perfect world. Adjust further down for fluffy product that won't pack a cavity.
