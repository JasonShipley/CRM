## Milestones and payment (about 10–12 weeks)
| # | Milestone | Timing | Payment |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Signed FEED agreement; kickoff and site visit | Week 0–2 | 50% ($64,975) with the signed agreement |
| 2 | Basis of design, PFDs, site master plan and preliminary GA layout issued for customer review | About week 5–6 | 25% ($32,488) net 10 from issue |

- This adds a mid-point payment to the standard FEED terms (50% down, balance net 10 on delivery). Use the standard two-step terms if Jason prefers.

## Deliverables
1. Basis of design: capacities, products and formulas, ingredient list, storage days, operating schedule
2. PFDs with mass, steam and utility balances
3. Site master plan for the tub plant and feed mill, with truck and rail traffic
4. 3D general arrangement, plan and elevation drawings
5. Equipment list and specifications; vendor quotes and bid tabs
6. Structural concept and foundation loads for the civil engineer
7. Motor and load list, electrical one-line concept, controls architecture and I/O count
8. Steam generator sizing, including whether the Farmers Rice 40 BHP Clayton is enough
9. Preliminary dust hazard analysis
10. Project schedule and firm price (+/-10%) for the build
