# Farmers Cooperative – Van Buren Feed Mill: budgetary proposal working notes

HubSpot deal 67884737544. Companion to the tub plant deal 66316562863 (MCE-Q-2610-FCO-R1).
Proposed ref: MCE-Q-2611-FCO-FM-R0 (budgetary)

## Basis of design (from Matt Crabtree, Nov 11, 2026)
- Replaces the Sallisaw mill: ~8,000 TPY of sweet and textured feed, all in 50 lb bags
- Pelleting adds 10–15k TPY (cubes, creep, layer pellets, economy rations), for 20–25k TPY total
- Peak Dec–Feb (Sep–Oct heavy in drought years): ~3,000–3,300 tons in the peak month
- ~10 ingredients, ~20 rations (10 made regularly), none medicated
- They buy a base pellet from outside today
- Material flows in screws up to the pellet mill; belts and drags after pelleting (pellet quality)

## Process flow (per Jason, Nov 20)
1. Truck receiving
2. Bulk storage
3. Grinding
4. Micro skid (quote Scherer)
5. Mixing
6. Surge bin
7. Pellet mill feeder
8. Conditioner
9. Pellet mill: Continental Agra Century, new. MCE cost $125,000 with feeder and conditioner
10. Belt conveyor to jump leg
11. Cooler
12. Drag conveyor
13. Screener
14. Loadout bins

Reference: the NEMO Feed pellet plant (Baring MO) for conveyors, leg, cooler, screener, drags and controls.

## Pricing rule
- Custom system: 50% margin (sell = 2× cost), uneven totals, round to $5
- Standard terms: 50% down, 30% before shipment, balance net 10
