Training Trip Overview

MCE Field Service Training Trip – Hammer Mill Operations Farmers Rice Milling Company will host Jason Bliss for a comprehensive 2-day on-site training program covering hammer mill operation, maintenance procedures, and troubleshooting. This training is designed for the Farmers Rice team at the Lake Charles, LA facility. The program includes detailed instruction on: • Hammer mill operation and safety protocols • Internal component inspection and maintenance • Common wear patterns and replacement procedures • Troubleshooting and diagnostic techniques Gordon Roberson has requested that a minimum of 6 team members attend the training to ensure comprehensive coverage of operations and maintenance responsibilities. All training materials and technical documentation will be provided by MCE. This quote covers the training service only and does not include repair work or parts. Optional repair services are available under a separate quote.
