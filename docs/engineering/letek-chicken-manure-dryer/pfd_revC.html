BGS-001Biogas conditioning skidFor 195 scfm at 500 ppm H2S or less: iron oxide vessels, gas cooler and knockout, blower, flame arresters, analyzers, control panel.
AutomationPLC / HMI with LPG and biogas fuel profiles, dryer temperature control, links to dewatering, skid and plant.

Basis: 128.7 MT/day of liquid digestate at 8% TS, 24 h/day; grit and sand handled separately. Dryer streams are from the MCE Dryer HMB Calculator (MCE-HMB-2026-002 Rev C) at full solids capture, the largest dryer load; air and LPG figures are on its propane basis. At typical press capture (60 to 80%) and 30 to 35% TS cake the exhaust is about 3,000 to 5,100 ACFM; the fan VFD holds the cyclone flow, and the cyclone and fan control are confirmed after the sample test, which also sets cake solids, solids capture and polymer demand. Liquid fraction composition is given in the proposal.
