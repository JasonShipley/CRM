Biomass Heat and Mass Balance Review

Doc. NT-HMB-2025-001 Rev 0 · Prozus Inc, 17 Jan 2025
Rotary dryer + thermal reactor, 3,000 lb/hr biomass · Engineering internal review · 8 Sep 2026
Bottom line the workbook holds two unrelated calculations and neither one closes
Prozus fuel figure
1.9× too high
290 kg/h biomass fuel (21% of feed) comes from dividing flue-gas mass by a stoichiometric flue-gas yield while treating that gas as 1,100°C. 47% of the fuel energy vanishes. Consistent figure: about 150 kg/h before fixing the heat demand, about 110 kg/h after.
MCE dryer sheet
Sized for 9,000 lb/hr, fed 1,333
The CALC heat block is pasted values from an earlier 9,000 lb/hr-evaporation job. Burner 15.7 MMBtu/hr, fan 31,564 ACFM, 36 in duct and 157 hp all inherit a 6.5× error. The sheet even flags itself: "INLET TEMP IS NOT CORRECT."
Product yield
67% is not credible
The reactor sheet takes biomass to 350°C with no mass loss except water. Torrefaction at that temperature sheds 25–30% of dry mass, so 912 kg/h should read roughly 640–690 kg/h.
Combustion chemistry
Sound in both tracks
Stoichiometric air 5.85 kg/kg (Prozus) and 5.82 lb/lb (MCE) for the same wood analysis; flue gas 8.95 kg/kg at 30% excess air; emission factors and airlock tables are fine.

The two tracks never meet. A "validation report" that does not reconcile the thing it validates has not validated it.

What to do with it

Send back to Prozus with findings 1, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8 and 11. Ask for one calculation, metric, with an energy closure line (fuel in = delivered + exhaust + losses) on each sheet and a stated reactor heating mode. The report is "Issued for Review" and reviewed/approved are blank ("NR"), so it has not been accepted; keep it that way.

Fix the MCE calculator (track B) before it is used on another quote: replace CALC F4:F15, H4, I39:I45, B47, D48 with formulas on Input, converge G67, pick one dryer model, and add a sanity line "Btu per lb water evaporated" that flags anything outside 1,400–2,200. FLOW G8 already computes it; it just does not shout.

Do not use any number from this file for burner, fan, duct or emissions until the two fixes above are in. The only figures safe to quote today are the feed mass balance and the combustion stoichiometry.

Not verified here: the hidden VBA module (not inspected), the Cost Sheet economics beyond noting they are dead, and whether the reactor is direct or indirect fired, which changes finding 6 from "impossible" to "mislabeled". Confidence in findings 1–4 is high; they are arithmetic. Findings 5 and 6 depend on what the ABTR reactor actually is.
