# Welcome to the MCE quote builder

You can now size and quote Midwest Custom Engineering equipment yourself, any time,
at **https://quotes.usemce.com**. Prices you see are MCE list prices.

## Signing in

1. Go to **quotes.usemce.com** and enter your work email, the one MCE set you up with.
2. We email you a six-digit code. Enter it. The code is good for 15 minutes.
3. You stay signed in on that device for 30 days. **Sign out** is in the menu at the top.

No password to remember. If the code doesn't arrive, check your spam folder, or ask
MCE to confirm the address we have for you.

## Put it on your iPhone

1. Open **quotes.usemce.com** in **Safari**.
2. Tap the **Share** button (the square with an arrow), then **Add to Home Screen**.
3. Open **MCE Quotes** from your Home Screen and sign in once. The app keeps its
   own sign-in, separate from Safari.

It opens full screen like any other app. If you switch to Mail to get your code,
it will still be waiting for the code when you come back. On a quote, **Share PDF**
sends the proposal by Mail, Messages or AirDrop, or saves it to Files.

## Building a quote

- **New quote**: describe the job in plain words, for example "4430 with a 200 HP
  motor grinding corn at 15 tons an hour through a 6/64 screen, with a plenum and an
  air system", and press **Build the quote**. Every line and price fills in, and you can
  change anything.
- **Save & open PDF** saves it and gives you the proposal to send your customer.
- Your quotes list shows everything your dealership has saved. Nobody outside your
  dealership sees your quotes.

Anything marked **needs MCE input** is an item we price by hand. Send us the quote
number and we'll fill it in.

## Talking to it

Press the orange **microphone** button at the bottom right and just talk:

- *"Size a cooler for two tons an hour of soybean meal coming in at 250 degrees."*
- *"What fan do I need for that?"*
- *"Quote Acme Feed a 4430, 200 horse, corn at 15 tons an hour, six sixty-fourths
  screen, with a plenum."*

It answers out loud and puts the results on your screen. Interrupt it whenever you
like. It builds a **draft** only. Nothing is saved or sent until you review it and
press Save. Every size and price comes from MCE's own calculators.

The first time, your browser will ask to use the microphone. Allow it. The button
works on phones too.

## Questions

Call or email MCE. For special pricing, freight or anything the builder can't answer,
we're happy to help.
