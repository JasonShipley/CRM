## Standard commercial terms

Every MCE quote and proposal uses these terms unless Jason gives different terms for that quote. When he does, use his terms for that quote only and keep the standard for the next one. Say in the summary which terms the quote carries.

### Payment terms (standard)

**50% down with order, 30% due before shipment, balance due net 10.**

Wording for the proposal's Commercial Terms section:

> Payment terms: 50% down with order; 30% due before shipment; balance due net 10. Payment terms are as stated on this quote and on the invoice. Past-due balances may be reported to business credit bureaus, including Dun & Bradstreet and Experian.
>
> Prices are in US dollars and exclude sales tax, freight and installation. Pricing is valid 30 days from the date of this proposal; steel and motor pricing are subject to adjustment for orders placed after that date. Warranty per MCE standard terms and conditions. This proposal covers the equipment listed in Section 2 only.

Show a milestone table with the dollar amount for each payment when the total is over $100,000.

### Sales tax

State the project's state by name: "Sales tax: this project is in [State] – [State] sales tax will be added at invoicing unless a valid [State] exemption certificate is provided before invoicing."

### Freight, delivery and installation

- **Freight:** FOB origin, MCE Stuart, FL or Newkirk, OK shop (assigned at order based on shop load and demand); freight quoted at time of shipment, or shipped freight collect.
- **Delivery:** lead time runs from receipt of order, approved drawings and down payment.
- **Approval drawings:** general arrangement drawings issued for customer approval before fabrication.
- **Installation:** by others. Do not include installation, millwright, rigging, electrical installation or crew travel in MCE's price unless Jason asks.
- **Start-up services:** $13,800 (QBO item "Onsite Commissioning Assistance") for MCE start-up assistance and operator training. Include it as a line item on complete systems and plants; offer it as an option on single machines. Additional days are at MCE's standard daily rate plus expenses.

### FEED agreements (custom plants and systems)

When a custom system needs front-end engineering before a firm price:

- FEED is a fixed price, stated in the proposal, and credited in full against the plant price when the plant is ordered.
- FEED payment: 50% down with the FEED agreement, balance net 10 on delivery of the FEED package.
- Proposal prices before FEED are budgetary; the FEED converts them to a firm price.

### Pricing guidelines

- Custom systems are priced at about 50% gross margin (sell price about 2x MCE cost). Jason may set a different price; flag the margin in the summary when the price comes in under 50%.
- Jason prefers uneven totals on large proposals (for example $1,758,850, not $1,750,000). When spreading a price change, scale every line by the same factor and round unit prices to the nearest $5.
- Do not offer royalty, revenue-share or financing structures in a written proposal unless Jason asks. Hold them back as negotiating levers.
- MCE's positioning is uptime: a separate maintenance and parts agreement on core components (hammer mills, coolers, cook systems, conveyors, drives) can be offered as a follow-on. Do not price it in the proposal unless asked.

### Reference

The wording above follows MCE proposals sent in October 2026 (e.g. MCEQ2610BIOURJA4436R2, MCE-Q-2610-NBO3-R3, MCE-Q-2610-FCO-R1).

## Add under "Escalate to a human"

- Customer asks for payment terms other than the standard 50% down / 30% before shipment / balance net 10, or for financing, royalty or extended terms.

## Add under "Never"

- Never change payment terms from the standard (or from terms Jason set for that quote) without Jason's approval.
